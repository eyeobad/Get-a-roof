Introduction to Crest App

Housing Costs Are High Enough Already

Across Nigeria, renters often pay more in agent fees and extra charges than the actual rent. Crest App changes this by enabling direct property access from verified owners with fewer charges and structured transactions.

01

Direct Listings

Landlords and property owners list directly, so renters avoid stacked agent fees and unnecessary middle costs.

02

Secure Transactions

Payments and agreements follow a monitored, structured process, so both parties transact with clarity and protection.
