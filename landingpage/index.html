Sorce - Tinder for Jobs

Sorce is tinder but for jobs. Since our inception in Aug. 2024, we've facilitated over 7.5 million swipes, helping candidates secure hundreds of interviews daily. Our users have received interviews & offers from prestigious companies such as Peloton, the University of Pennsylvania, Vercel, Robinhood, Geico, Red Bull, and Bank of America.

Our app currently hosts 1.6 million jobs, and this number is growing rapidly. After you upload your resume, you can swipe right on jobs you want and swipe left on the jobs you don't want. When a user swipes right, our AI Agent navigates to the company's website and applies on their behalf.

Sorce caters to job seekers across the entire employment spectrum - from entry-level retail positions to senior executive roles in software engineering, marketing, and sales. Our platform serves everyone, regardless of industry or experience level.

How Sorce Helps Job Seekers

We started Sorce to make applying to jobs less painful and more delightful. This is the philosophy that guides all our decisions. From the AI Agent that helps job applicants apply, to the tinder experience, to the colors in the app.

Sorce has helped tens of thousands of people streamline their job search process, making it faster, easier, and more 'demure' than ever before.

Sorce has helped people land interviews at companies like SpaceX, Twitch, MongoDB, Neuralink, Jane Street, Circle, Canonical, Virtu Financial, C3AI, Mercury, Bubble.io, Astranis, Scale AI, Anduril, and Zipline.
